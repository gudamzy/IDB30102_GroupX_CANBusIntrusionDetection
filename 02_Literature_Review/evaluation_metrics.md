Evaluation metrics identified from previous research
Metric	Used by	Issue found	Used in this study
Accuracy	Almost all reviewed studies	Stays high on imbalanced data even when attacks are missed	Yes (reported, not the main measure)
Precision, recall, F1-score	Almost all	Window-level in some studies (Cheng et al., 2022)	Yes, per message
False-positive rate	Few studies; Ben Othmane et al. (2022) report 42.5–68% on a real vehicle	Often not reported	Yes, FP / (FP + TN)
Cross-vehicle performance	Xiao et al. (2024); Lampe & Meng (2024)	Rare	Yes, F1 retention = cross F1 / same F1
