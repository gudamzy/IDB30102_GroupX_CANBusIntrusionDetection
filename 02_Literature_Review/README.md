# 02_Literature_Review

This folder contains supporting materials for Chapter 2 of the research proposal. The content is aligned with Chapter 2. If the literature review is updated, the supporting files in this folder should also be updated to maintain consistency.

## Folder Contents

| File | Content |
|---|---|
| `literature_review_table.md` | Table 2.1 from the proposal and the SLR thematic synthesis |
| `technique_comparison.md` | Comparison of existing techniques and reasons for selecting Random Forest, Decision Tree, and Support Vector Machine |
| `gap_analysis.md` | The two research gaps identified in Section 2.9 and their mapping to the research objectives |
| `datasets.md` | CAN bus datasets identified from the literature and the reason for selecting the can-train-and-test dataset |
| `evaluation_metrics.md` | Evaluation metrics identified from previous studies and the metrics selected for this research |
| `methodology_references.md` | References supporting the selected experimental research methodology |

## Purpose

These supporting materials provide evidence for the selection of the dataset, machine learning models, evaluation metrics, and experimental methodology used in the proposed research.

They also help maintain traceability between the literature review, identified research gaps, research objectives, and the proposed methodology.
